# SnagList

> Defect tracking for small construction and renovation contractors.

SnagList turns every defect found at the end of a construction or renovation job into a trackable item with its location, before/after photos, an assigned subcontractor, and a deadline, so jobs get closed out faster and with less back-and-forth.

---

## Vision

### Vision Statement

> **FOR** small construction and renovation contractors **WHO** need to track and close out dozens of defects across several subcontractors at the end of every job, **THE** SnagList is a mobile-first, web-based defect tracking service **THAT** turns every defect into a trackable item with its location, before/after photos, an assigned subcontractor, and a deadline, so jobs are closed out faster, disputes are settled with evidence, and final payments arrive sooner. **UNLIKE** enterprise construction platforms such as PlanRadar and Fieldwire, which are priced and designed for large firms with dedicated project managers, **OUR PRODUCT** can be learned in minutes, lets subcontractors and clients take part through a simple link without creating an account, and is affordable for a one-person contractor.

### Breakdown (Moore's Vision Template)

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **FOR** (target customer) | Independent builders and small renovation firms, typically one owner coordinating a handful of subcontractors across a few active projects at a time |
| **WHO** (need or opportunity) | Lose time and money at the end of each job because unfinished work and quality issues are scattered across paper notes, camera rolls, and messenger chats, leading to missed fixes, repeat site visits, and delayed final payments |
| **THE** (product name) **is a** (product category) | SnagList, a defect-tracking app that runs in the browser and is designed primarily for use on a phone at the job site |
| **THAT** (key benefit) | Gives each issue a single record with photo evidence, a pinned location, a responsible person, and a due date, so nothing is forgotten, everyone knows what they owe, and the client signs off on a documented, complete job |
| **UNLIKE** (primary competitive alternative) | Professional field-management suites built for large general contractors, which require training, per-seat licenses, and an office team to run them |
| **OUR PRODUCT** (primary differentiation) | Fits the way small teams already work: subcontractors and clients join by opening a shared link, the contractor is productive on the first project, and the price is set for a business of one |
